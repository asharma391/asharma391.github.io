Arjun Sharma

I'm studying computer science and neuroscience at the University of Toronto, where I'm a National Scholar.

In summer 2026, I worked with Dr. Mélanie Courtot at the Ontario Institute for Cancer Research on TreeRAG, an approach to information retrieval that navigates a hierarchy instead of using vector search. I've also worked on transfer learning and knowledge distillation to reduce diagnostic bias at U of T, and on the effects of environmental conditions on COPD at Ontario Tech.

In 2024, I represented Canada at the International Biology Olympiad and received an honourable mention. In high school, I founded our computer science club and built the student government election platform, used by more than 600 voters each year.

Outside of research, I've spent time playing violin, competing in debate, and tutoring. I've served as a concertmaster for the CISMF National Ensemble and as an elected councillor on Victoria University's student council.
